Chats

to
Every contact already gets built-in instructions to text like a real person (short, casual, no AI-speak). Pick “Custom only” in a contact's settings to turn that off for them.

Models

How much the model can read at once: chat history plus any PDFs or documents you send. Bigger means better recall and longer documents, but slower replies and more RAM. With 4,096, roughly the first 3-4 pages of a PDF fit.

Web

Type /search something in any chat and the contact looks it up and answers. Searches and opened links are the only things that leave your machine.

Needs json enabled under search.formats in its settings.yml.

Data

Everything is stored locally in a SQLite file next to the app. Nothing leaves your machine except requests to your Ollama server.
